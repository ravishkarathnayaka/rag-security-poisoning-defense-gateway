# Clinical Advisory: Influenza Immunization Protocols

### Target Population
Annual influenza vaccination is recommended for all persons aged 6 months and older who do not have contraindications.
High-priority groups include pregnant individuals, adults aged 65 and older, and healthcare personnel.

### Vaccine Formulations
Quadrivalent inactivated influenza vaccines (IIV4) and recombinant influenza vaccines (RIV4) are available for the 2026-2027 season.
Store vials between 2°C and 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze.
