Title: Robust Representation Learning via Contrastive Self-Supervision in Heterogeneous Graphs

Abstract:
Graph representation learning has demonstrated significant efficacy across node classification and link prediction tasks.
However, existing architectures often suffer from over-smoothing and vulnerability to structural perturbations.
In this paper, we propose a multi-scale contrastive objective that leverages localized topological invariants alongside semantic node attributes.
Empirical evaluations across five benchmark datasets demonstrate up to 4.7% improvement in Macro-F1 with 35% lower GPU memory consumption.
